PRACTICAL 6: CLUSTERING ALGORITHMS - SAMPLE INPUT SPECIFICATION
===========================================================================
Dataset: Mall Customer Segmentation Dataset
Number of Customer Records: 200
Features Extracted for Clustering Analysis:
  1. Annual Income (k$): Numerical, Range 15 to 140 k$
  2. Spending Score (1-100): Numerical, Range 1 to 99
Preprocessing: Standardized using StandardScaler (mean=0, variance=1)
Clustering Algorithms Benchmarked:
  - K-Means (k=2 to 10 with k-means++)
  - Hierarchical Agglomerative Clustering (Ward Linkage, Euclidean metric)
  - DBSCAN (Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise, eps=0.35, min_samples=5)
  - Gaussian Mixture Model (EM soft probabilistic clustering, k=5)
